Turn a Shopify payout CSV into a clean reconciliation breakdown.

Drop your Shopify Payments transactions export here or click to choose · Finance › Payouts › View transactions › Export · CSV, UTF-8

What it does

  • Groups every transaction under its payout using the Payout Date (and Payout ID when your export has one) and the currency.
  • Adds up what the export says — charges, refunds, adjustments, disputes, fees — in exact integer cents, and shows the sum of Amount − Fee and the sum of the Net column, so you can see when Shopify's own columns disagree.
  • Checks the payout total when you add the payouts export: MATCHED, or the exact signed DIFFERENCE. Two payouts on the same day in the same currency are reported as AMBIGUOUS rather than guessed.
  • Keeps provenance. The detail export carries the source file, row, raw value and the transformation for every amount.

What it will not do

  • It never treats a missing value as zero. Anything the export does not carry is shown as not available from source.
  • It does not calculate VAT or tax. A VAT column, if present, is passed through exactly as Shopify exported it.
  • It does not decide accounting treatment, post to QuickBooks or Xero, or match your bank statement. It organises and reconciles the source data so that step is quick.
  • It does not read or change your store. It only reads files you chose.
